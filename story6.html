I wish I could've told you this before.

But I didn't.

When you left, you said some words — and those words never left my head. That's the only sound I hear now, whether I'm alone or standing in a room full of people talking to me. Your voice is the only thing that gets through.

And the truth is, I don't want it to stop. Because maybe your voice is the only piece of you I have left.

Some of what you said were lies — just to keep me away from you. Yeah, it hurt. But not the way a lie is supposed to hurt. It hurts because I made you feel like you had to lie to me in the first place. I'm sorry. Maybe it wouldn't sting as much if those lies were meant to wound me — but they weren't. They came from you not wanting to hurt me, only wanting to be far from me.

I know my vibes are different. I know I'm a little strange. I know you had someone else to look after instead of me. I know I was always too quiet, or too loud, never quite saying enough. It wasn't that you hated it — you just never liked it either.

I wish I'd told you this before. Maybe then you would've stayed.

I just want you to know — I was never too quiet. I was always talking to you, just inside my own head, thinking somehow you could hear it, forgetting that no one can read minds. I was the one who needed to learn that. And I was never too loud — if anything, I was too quiet, saying everything only to myself, never out loud to you.

I wish I could go back. I'd read your mind instead, and become whoever you needed me to be.

I thought I could just be myself with you — that I wouldn't have to guess what you were thinking, that I wouldn't have to live scared that one wrong guess would make you leave.

But I was wrong the whole time.

Whatever I can say now is…
