The Last Thoughts of a Dying Star

That one night I always fear is tonight.

Tonight, I am dying. I don't know where I will fall here when I am dying, and that's the worst thing that could happen to me in my whole life. When I am crying and dying, I am shining more here. That's the saddest thing that could happen to me at the same moment that is the lucky day for someone. They are making wishes after they see me dying. Is Allah/God gonna do what they wish for? Do we have to die to make someone or humans happy? So that they can make wishes? Why, Allah/God?

Why is it like this? I wish I could stay in this sky longer. Other stars say, "Think good. You are making someone happy when you are dying," but they are brainwashed. At least, that's what I think. They never think about how sad and bad this system is for us.

Nobody cares for us. They are busy making wishes for their own good, but nobody makes a wish for our peace.

How sad.
