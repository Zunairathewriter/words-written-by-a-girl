I Built a Website Before I Learned Web Development, Here's How

No coding classes. No bootcamp. Just curiosity, ChatGPT, and DeepSeek.

Hello, readers! Today I'm going to tell you how and why I created a website just for my articles.

How did I create a website without knowing a single thing about web development?

AI helped me.

I'm 13 years old, and I always want to discover something new. Always.

So one day I was bored. I wanted to write something, but my mind was like a trash can full of useless papers, so I asked ChatGPT what the hottest topic of 2026 is. Yes, I want everyone to read my articles, which is why I wanted the hottest topic.

The first thing that came to mind was Arirang. Many of you are probably wondering, "What is Arirang?" Well, just go and search for it! It's BTS's comeback album. Of course everyone knows BTS. In the end, they're all over the world.

ChatGPT gave me a lot of topics, but my trash can didn't like any of them. As we kept chatting, the idea of creating a website only for my articles crossed my mind. We chatted about it a lot, and then I thought:

Well, my dear boring times, you're going far away for a long time.

That's how I discovered web development. But something was worrying me: I didn't know a single thing about it. So I told myself, "Let's just get started." Then I asked ChatGPT so many random questions, and I mean really random ones.

After a lot of hard work, an issue came up: the issue of highlighting. That issue of highlighting was eating my brain. ChatGPT tried very hard to help, but it couldn't fix it.

So one day I decided to visit DeepSeek, and the next day I did. I told DeepSeek everything, and it said the issue could be solved. I asked if we could delete my website and rebuild it from scratch, and DeepSeek said, "Why not?" So I deleted my website and did it all again. It was brain-eating because DeepSeek wasn't good at CSS at first. I showed it screenshots of my old website's CSS, and then DeepSeek got it right.

I even cried while doing this, because I was on my period and DeepSeek was eating my brain, so it all came out.

After a lot of hard work, I finished my website in one month. It still doesn't have a logo, because I haven't chosen one yet.

I didn't post about my website anywhere. But I asked my favorite writer to review it, and he did. He said it was all very good and that I should keep it simple and not add anything, except an "About Me" section. And I did add an "About Me" section.

So, do you want to see a website created by a 13-year-old girl and find out what she thinks at this age? Well, let me tell you: I was hit by reality too early, so you're going to relate to every word (at least, that's what I think). On my website, you'll find short stories, some poems (at least, I think they're poems), and some articles you can relate to.

After I finished my website, I started to like web development. It's fun! Before building it, I didn't know anything about web development. I only learned what HTML, CSS, and JavaScript mean after I finished my website. So I started learning web development from ChatGPT, and it's going well now.

This story tells you that if you want to do something, go ahead and do it. Don't wait until you've learned everything. You can learn while you keep going. Don't waste your time.

Or maybe we do have some time to waste. Maybe sometimes it's important to waste your time.
