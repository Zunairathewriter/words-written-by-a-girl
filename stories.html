The World Behind Closed Eyes

There's a whole universe behind closed eyes, my dear.

Close your eyes and see it.

Feel the colors that don't have names.

Hear the silence that speaks louder than words.

Every eyelash is a reminder that you can shut out the noise and find peace within.

Your Stranger

You hold me for once and for all, my love.

In a crowd of familiar faces, you were the stranger who felt like home.

One touch, one glance, and the universe rearranged itself around us.

Now I search for you in every room, every street, every heartbeat.

And I find you — in the spaces between my thoughts.
