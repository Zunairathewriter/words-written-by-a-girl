Every story begins with a feeling. But mine begins with my thoughts.

As soft as cream

As green as grass

As dark as the night sky

As shining as a diamond

As fresh as morning

As light as the summer's clothes

As big as the world

I can move or not

I can breathe or not

But I will always be yours

Doesn't matter

If you are the one who kills me or not

But I will always love you the same way

Like a mad lover
