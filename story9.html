Your eyes are the camera

And your heart is the memory card

There are always a lot of moments in my life when I wish I had a camera because there are always a lot of good moments in my life, like my kittens playing with each other so well that I want to shoot them so badly!

Sometimes, when Bruce is sleeping in the sunshine, I want to take a pic with a camera and save it on the memory card of the camera (that I don't have).

Sometimes, when Leela is sleeping or staring so cutely, I want to take a pic with a camera and save it on the memory card of the camera (that I don't have).

Sometimes, Fairy plays so hard that she breathes with her mouth open, and I want to take a pic with a camera and save it on the memory card of my camera (that I don't have).

Sometimes, Ruby aims so well that I want to take a pic with a camera and save it on the memory card of my camera (that I don't have).

And today, one of my cats was sitting in a spot where sunlight was landing. Tehreem said (Tehreem is my twin sis), "I wish I had a camera. I would take his pic."

And by this statement of hers, I was reminded of all the moments when I wished for the same, all those moments when those memories flashed in front of my eyes. And I thought, all those memories are already saved in my heart.

I told her that you already do have a camera.

"Your eyes are the camera, and your heart is the memory card."

Isn't it weird?

And there are people who someday lose their camera or their memory card, but I will pray for all the good people that they don't lose their camera or their memory card.

That's when I realized my camera already takes good pics without any filters, and my memory card is full of memories, but there are never enough memories to fill the memory card.

I emptied my memory card the moment my kids (my cats) entered my life and filled it with only their memories.

Now that memory card is not mine anymore. Now it's theirs.
